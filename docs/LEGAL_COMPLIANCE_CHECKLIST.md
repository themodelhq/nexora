# Nexora — Legal & Compliance Checklist

> **Disclaimer:** This is not legal advice and does not constitute a legal
> conclusion. Nexora makes no claim that the token or any sale is "legally
> compliant." Qualified legal counsel must review all of the following areas.

## Areas requiring qualified legal review

- [ ] **Token classification** — whether NXR is a utility token, a security,
      a commodity, or something else in each relevant jurisdiction.
- [ ] **Public sale / presale** — whether the sale structure triggers
      securities registration, exemptions, or a ban.
- [ ] **Marketing** — whether any website, whitepaper, or social copy
      constitutes an offer of securities or misleading advertising.
- [ ] **Securities considerations** — Howey / equivalent tests; profit
      expectation; reliance on the efforts of others.
- [ ] **AML / KYC** — requirements for any fiat/asset on-ramp or sale; wallet
      screening; sanctions screening.
- [ ] **Consumer protection** — fair dealing, refunds, cancellation rights,
      clear risk disclosure.
- [ ] **Data protection** — GDPR and equivalents; processing of wallet
      addresses, IP addresses, email; consent for notifications.
- [ ] **Tax** — tax treatment of the token, airdrops, staking rewards, and any
      sale in each jurisdiction.
- [ ] **Geographic restrictions** — which jurisdictions may not participate.
- [ ] **Sanctions** — OFAC and other sanctions-list screening of users.
- [ ] **Advertising** — truthful, non-misleading claims; no guaranteed profits.
- [ ] **Exchange requirements** — listing/application requirements for each
      platform (see `LISTING_CHECKLIST.md`).

## Nexora's standing commitments

- Nexora does **not** claim guaranteed profits, guaranteed returns, or
  guaranteed listings.
- The presale is **disabled by default** and only enabled after legal review.
- No false regulatory assurances are made anywhere in the project.
