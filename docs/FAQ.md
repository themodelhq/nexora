# Nexora — FAQ

**Is NXR a guaranteed investment?**
No. NXR is a utility token. Cryptocurrency markets are volatile and
participation involves risk of loss. Nexora makes no profit or listing
guarantees.

**Can the team mint more NXR?**
No. The supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 and there is no mint function. The
maximum supply is enforced on-chain.

**Can anyone steal or modify my balance?**
No. The token has no owner confiscation, no blacklist, no hidden taxes. Balances
change only via ERC-20 transfers.

**How do I claim the airdrop?**
Connect your wallet, verify eligibility, and claim. Claims are verified by
cryptographic Merkle proofs on-chain; double claims are impossible.

**Is there a lockup?**
Team, advisor, partner and grant allocations vest on-chain (cliff + linear
release). See `docs/VESTING.md`.

**Where are the funds?**
Treasury funds are controlled by a multisig + timelock, not a single key. All
addresses are public (Transparency page).

**Do I ever need to share my seed phrase / private key?**
Never. Nexora never requests seed phrases or private keys. Authentication uses
wallet signatures.

**Is the contract audited?**
The code uses audited OpenZeppelin primitives and has passed automated tests
and static analysis, but a full independent third-party audit is **not yet
complete**. See `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.

**Will NXR be listed on exchanges?**
We do not promise any listing. Listings are announced only if/when actually
approved.
